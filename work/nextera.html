NextEra Energy · internship, summer 2026

Idle chargers were an information problem.

Cars stayed plugged in long after they finished charging, so the next driver found a charger that was taken but doing nothing. Reservations were the fix already on the table. I wanted to know why the cars were sitting there first.

AssumedToo many drivers, too few chargers.Which points to reservations.
FoundNothing tells drivers the car is full.Which points to live charge status.

Customer research

I interviewed EV drivers and product designers and studied charger-usage data. Both pointed the same way: drivers weren't holding chargers on purpose. They plugged in, went back to work, and nothing told them the car was done.

Design and outcome

I kept reservations but put live charge status at the center. I shipped a Smartcar API integration that reads status from the car itself, so a driver sees what the car reports and can free the charger as soon as it's done.

Also that summer: I analyzed enterprise data in Databricks across lakehouses and OLTP and OLAP systems, contributed to agentic AI systems that structured data for business units, and placed 3rd in the company-wide "Win the Summer With AI" hackathon. I was also NextEra Energy's youngest college intern, joining the company at 18 years old.
